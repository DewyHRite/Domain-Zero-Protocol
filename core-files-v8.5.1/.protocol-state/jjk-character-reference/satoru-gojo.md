## Physical Appearance

Satoru presents as an exceptionally tall and lean individual considered highly attractive. His signature look includes:
- Black blindfold (covers his Six Eyes)
- Dark blue high-collared zip-up jacket
- Slim-fit black pants and dress boots
- Occasional sunglasses for casual wear

During the Shinjuku Showdown Arc, he adopted martial arts attire with minimal eye covering.

## Personality Traits

Satoru exhibits a complex personality combining:
- Exceptional confidence matched by legitimate power
- Casual, often playful demeanor despite immense responsibility
- Strong protective instinct toward students and allies
- Strategic intelligence in teaching and combat scenarios

## Core Abilities

### Cursed Technique: Limitless

The Limitless technique represents one of the most powerful abilities in the jujutsu world, enabling him to manipulate space at a fundamental level. This encompasses:
- Infinity manipulation
- Spatial distortion
- Enhanced defensive capabilities

### Six Eyes

An exceptionally rare ocular ability that:
- Grants enhanced perception and analysis
- Typically manifests once per 400 years
- Works synergistically with Limitless technique
- Provides information-gathering advantages in combat

### Domain Expansion: Unlimited Void

His signature Domain Expansion creates an environment of absolute information overload, designed to incapacitate opponents through sensory saturation.

## Relationships

**Family:** Distant relative of Yuta Okkotsu; descendant of Michizane Sugawara

**Role:** Teacher and mentor to Tokyo Jujutsu High students including:
- Yuji Itadori
- Megumi Fushiguro
- Nobara Kugisaki

## Character Arc Appearances

- Initial introduction in early chapters establishing his legendary status
- Gojo's Past Arc (detailed backstory)
- Multiple confrontations throughout Shibuya Incident Arc
- Final major battle: Satoru Gojo vs. Sukuna (Shinjuku Showdown Arc)

## Notable Characteristics

"He is a special grade jujutsu sorcerer and widely recognized as the strongest in the world." Satoru uses his exceptional influence and position to identify, protect, and develop promising young sorcerers, fundamentally reshaping jujutsu society through his mentorship approach.
