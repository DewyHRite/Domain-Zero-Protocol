## Basic Information

**Full Name:** Maki Zenin (禪院真希)

**Aliases:** "Big Sis" (used by Mai)

**Birthday:** January 20, 2002

**Age:** 16 years old (currently); 15 in prequel series

**Gender:** Female

**Height:** 170 cm (5'7")

**Species:** Human

## Physical Appearance

**Hair:** Black (manga), Dark Green (anime)

**Eyes:** Green (manga), Amber (anime)

Maki maintains a athletic, muscular build befitting her combat-focused role. Post-Shibuya incident, her appearance undergoes significant changes reflecting her character transformation.

## Family & Relationships

**Parents:** Ogi Zenin (father), unnamed mother

**Siblings:** Mai Zenin (younger twin sister)

**Extended Family:** Naobito Zenin (uncle), Naoya Zenin (cousin), Jinichi Zenin (cousin), Toji Fushiguro (cousin)

**Romantic Partner:** Yuta Okkotsu (husband)

**Descendants:** One daughter, with grandchildren Tsurugi and Yuka Okkotsu (in Modulo timeline)

## Professional Status

**Occupation:** Jujutsu Sorcerer and student

**Affiliation:** Tokyo Jujutsu High School

**Rank:** Grade 4

**Former Affiliation:** Zenin Clan (expulsed)

## Character Overview

Maki functions as "a major supporting character" and "one of the main protagonists of its prequel." Born into the prestigious Zenin Clan despite lacking innate sorcerous ability, she endured systematic mistreatment. Her motivation stems from proving herself through jujutsu mastery, driven by spite toward her family's rejection.

## Personality Traits

- **Determination:** Relentless pursuit of sorcerous excellence despite initial disadvantages
- **Rebellious Spirit:** Consciously rejected clan expectations and traditions
- **Mentor Figure:** Serves as role model for first-year students
- **Loyalty:** Works closely with close friends Panda and Toge Inumaki
- **Competitive Nature:** Uses familial opposition as fuel for achievement

## Abilities & Powers

### Overview
Despite lacking cursed energy perception abilities common to sorcerers, Maki compensates through exceptional physical training and mastery of conventional combat techniques.

### Physical Prowess
Her combat effectiveness relies on:
- Exceptional hand-to-hand combat skills
- Superior strength and agility
- Weapon proficiency
- Tactical fighting intelligence

### Cursed Energy Deficiency
Maki cannot naturally perceive or manipulate cursed energy, creating her foundational disadvantage within the jujutsu world.

### Heavenly Restriction
Her condition represents a "Heavenly Restriction"—a paradoxical jujutsu concept granting enhanced physical capabilities in exchange for cursed energy inability. This restriction becomes central to her character arc and power development.

## Character Development

Maki's journey involves transforming her perceived weakness into strategic advantage. Her post-Shibuya character evolution reflects significant narrative progression, though specific details remain spoiler-sensitive.

## Notable Narrative Roles

- **Prequel Prominence:** Central protagonist in *Jujutsu Kaisen 0: Jujutsu High*
- **Series Progression:** Secondary but significant character in main series
- **Arc Participation:** Involvement in major story arcs including Shibuya Incident
- **Alternate Timeline:** Deceased in *Jujutsu Kaisen Modulo* (sequel series)

## First Appearances

**Manga:** Chapter 0-1 (prequel), Chapter 10 (main series)

**Anime:** Episode 5
