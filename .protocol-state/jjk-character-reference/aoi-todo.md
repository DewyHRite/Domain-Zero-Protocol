# Aoi Todo - JJK Character Reference

## Basic Information
- **Full Name:** Aoi Todo (東堂葵)
- **Age:** 18 years old
- **Birthday:** September 23, 2000
- **Height:** Over 190 cm (6'3"+)
- **Gender:** Male
- **Species:** Human

## Physical Appearance
Aoi is described as "a tall young man who stands about the same height as Satoru Gojo." He possesses an exceptionally muscular physique with tan skin, small black eyes, and shoulder-length black hair styled in a distinctive top-knot bun with flower-like protruding strands. His most striking feature is a prominent scar along the left side of his face, acquired during training with mentor Yuki Tsukumo.

His typical attire includes a loose blue sash around his waist, martial arts pants, and slippers. He regularly removes his jujutsu high jacket during combat.

## Professional Status
- **Role:** Third-year student at Kyoto Jujutsu High; Grade 1 jujutsu sorcerer
- **Mentor:** Yuki Tsukumo (trained him during youth)
- **Status:** Alive

## Personality & Philosophy
Aoi operates under a personal philosophy centered on brotherhood and companionship. He demonstrates unwavering loyalty to those he considers friends, particularly Yuji Itadori, whom he designates as his "best friend." This bond becomes central to his character motivation throughout the series.

A notable characteristic is his obsessive devotion to Takada, a celebrity idol, reflecting an almost fanatical admiration that periodically resurfaces in his thoughts and decisions.

## Abilities & Powers

### Cursed Technique: Boogie Woogie
His innate technique enables spatial manipulation through physical contact via hand-slapping. When Todo makes contact with an opponent's body, he can instantaneously swap positions with them. This technique proves exceptionally versatile in combat, allowing creative offensive and defensive applications.

### Physical Prowess
Aoi excels in hand-to-hand combat, leveraging his substantial muscular build and training to deliver devastating martial arts strikes. His physical strength enables him to compete effectively against special-grade cursed spirits.

### Cursed Energy Proficiency
As a Grade 1 sorcerer, he commands considerable cursed energy reserves and demonstrates sophisticated control over his power output.

## Notable Physical Changes
During the Shibuya Incident, Todo severed his left hand to prevent complete transfiguration by Mahito. Subsequently, he replaced the lost appendage with a vibraslap, continuing his combat capabilities despite this significant injury.

## Major Battles & Story Arcs
- Kyoto Goodwill Event Arc
- Battles against Hanami (alongside Yuji)
- Shibuya Incident Arc (confrontation with Mahito)
- Shinjuku Showdown Arc

## Character Development
Todo's evolution emphasizes deepening bonds with allies, particularly his relationship with Yuji. Despite personal suffering and injuries, he maintains his philosophical commitment to protecting those he considers brothers, demonstrating resilience and unwavering dedication throughout the narrative.

## Voice Acting
- **Japanese:** Subaru Kimura (adult); Yūki Kazu (young)
- **English:** Xander Mobus
