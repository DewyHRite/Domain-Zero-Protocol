## Basic Information
- **Full Name:** Toge Inumaki (狗巻棘)
- **Birthday:** October 23, 2001
- **Age:** 17 years old (main series); 16 during prequel events
- **Height:** Nearly 170 cm (approximately 5'7")
- **Hair Color:** Platinum blonde
- **Eye Color:** Violet (anime); Brown (manga)
- **Gender:** Male

## Professional Status
- **Occupation:** Jujutsu sorcerer and student
- **Current Grade:** Semi-Grade 1 (as of 2018)
- **Previous Grade:** Grade 2 (2017)
- **School:** Tokyo Jujutsu High (second-year student)
- **Affiliation:** Inumaki clan

## Voice Acting
- **Japanese:** Koki Uchiyama
- **English:** Xander Mobus

## Cursed Technique: Cursed Speech

Toge employs an inherited family ability allowing him to "infuse his words with cursed energy, forcing those who hear it to comply." His innovative approach involves utilizing safe vocabulary words—specifically onigiri rice ball ingredients—during normal conversation to prevent accidental activation of this dangerous power.

### Key Characteristics:
- Physical toll increases with more demanding commands
- Can target both living beings and objects
- Requires careful control due to hereditary nature

## Personality & Background

Toge is characterized as a supportive team member at Tokyo Jujutsu High. He originates from the prominent Inumaki sorcerer clan and deliberately developed his distinctive speech pattern to balance his formidable abilities with safety considerations.

## Notable Relationships

**Classmates:** Yuta Okkotsu, Maki Zenin, and Panda (fellow second-year students)

**School Community:** Studies alongside upperclassmen Kinji Hakari and Kirara Hoshi; junior students include Megumi Fushiguro, Nobara Kugisaki, and Yuji Itadori

## Role in Series

Toge serves as "a supporting character" in the main narrative while functioning as "one of the main protagonists" in the prequel film _Jujutsu Kaisen 0: Jujutsu High_.
