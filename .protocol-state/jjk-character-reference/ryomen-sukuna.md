## Physical Appearance

Sukuna possesses a distinctive appearance in both his host form and true incarnation:
- Four arms (true form)
- Two faces (back-to-back, true form)
- Black markings/tattoos covering body when in control
- Red irises when manifesting through host
- Imposing, regal bearing reflecting his status as King

When incarnated in Megumi Fushiguro:
- Adopted Megumi's physical form
- Black lines/markings appeared on face and body
- Maintained characteristic arrogance in posture

## Personality Traits

Sukuna exhibits a complex personality combining:
- Absolute confidence bordering on arrogance (justified by power)
- Cruel enjoyment of others' suffering
- Surprisingly principled adherence to his own rules
- Grudging respect for worthy opponents
- Disdain for weakness but appreciation for potential
- Strategic patience when it serves his goals

## Core Abilities

### Cursed Technique: Dismantle and Cleave

Sukuna's signature techniques represent overwhelming offensive power:
- **Dismantle**: Slashing attack for objects without cursed energy
- **Cleave**: Adjustable attack that adapts to target's cursed energy
- **Spiderweb**: Domain-enhanced version creating inescapable slashes

### Fire Arrow (Fuga)

A fire-based cursed technique producing devastating flame attacks:
- Creates arrows of intense flame
- Can be enhanced within Domain Expansion
- Demonstrates versatility beyond cutting techniques

### Domain Expansion: Malevolent Shrine

Sukuna's unique Domain Expansion:
- Does NOT create a separate barrier space
- Instead, manifests a Buddhist shrine that affects real-world surroundings
- Considered the pinnacle of Domain Expansion technique
- Range extends approximately 200 meters
- Continuous slashing attacks on all within range

## Relationship with Gojo

**Central Rivalry**: Sukuna and Gojo represent the ultimate opposing forces:

- Gojo: "The Strongest" of the modern era
- Sukuna: "The Strongest" of all time
- Their final battle determined the fate of jujutsu society
- Mutual recognition of each other's power
- Adversarial respect despite opposing goals

**Final Battle (Shinjuku Showdown Arc)**:
- Extended confrontation showcasing both at peak power
- Resulted in Sukuna's ultimate defeat by collaborative effort
- Demonstrated neither could easily overcome the other

## Character Arc Appearances

- Introduction through Yuji consuming finger
- Multiple manifestations during Cursed Training Arc
- Shibuya Incident Arc (major actions)
- Incarnation into Megumi Fushiguro
- Final battles in Shinjuku Showdown Arc
- Conclusive confrontation with combined sorcerer forces

## Notable Characteristics

"Ryomen Sukuna is a legendary Cursed Spirit widely considered to be the undisputed King of Curses and was one of the primary antagonists of the Jujutsu Kaisen series."

Sukuna's existence challenged fundamental assumptions about power dynamics in jujutsu society. His return forced cooperation among traditionally divided factions, ultimately proving that even the King of Curses could be overcome through collaboration and sacrifice.
