## Basic Information

**Full Name:** Yuji Itadori (虎杖悠仁)

**Age:** 15 years old

**Birthday:** March 20, 2003

**Gender:** Male

**Height:** 173 cm (5'8")

**Weight:** 80 kg (176 lb)

**Species:** Human

## Physical Appearance

Yuji possesses an average build with a lean, muscular physique beneath baggy clothing. He is distinguished by relatively large light brown eyes and distinctive spiky pink hair (brownish blonde in manga) styled in an undercut fashion. After consuming one of Sukuna's fingers, he develops a second pair of eyes that emerge when his curse tenant controls his body, accompanied by numerous markings across his form.

## Family Relations

- **Parents:** Jin Itadori (father), Kaori Itadori (mother)
- **Grandfather:** Wasuke Itadori
- **Half-Brothers:** Choso, Eso, Kechizu, and several others (through Kenjaku connection)

## Personal Status

**Current Role:**
- Main protagonist of Jujutsu Kaisen
- First-year student at Tokyo Metropolitan Curse Technical College
- Jujutsu Sorcerer

**Status:** Alive

## Background

Yuji lived an ordinary life in Sendai City until encountering Megumi Fushiguro and consuming a finger belonging to Ryomen Sukuna. This pivotal action transformed him into a vessel for the curse, prompting his enrollment at Tokyo Jujutsu High where he studies alongside Megumi and Nobara Kugisaki.

## Key Characteristics

**Personality Traits:**
The character displays a determined and compassionate nature, willing to engage with the supernatural world despite its dangers.

**Notable Aliases:**
- Sukuna's Vessel
- The Tiger of West Junior High
- "Yuu-chan" (per Kirara Hoshi)
- "Brat" (per Sukuna)
- "Brother" (per Aoi Todo)

## Professional Status

**Affiliated Organization:** Tokyo Metropolitan Curse Technical College
