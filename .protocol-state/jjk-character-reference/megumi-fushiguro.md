## Basic Information

**Full Name:** Megumi Fushiguro (伏黒恵)

**Role:** Deuteragonist of Jujutsu Kaisen series

**Age:** 15-16 years old

**Birthday:** December 22, 2002

**Height:** 175 cm (5'9")

**Weight:** 60 kg (132 lbs)

**Gender:** Male

**Physical Appearance:** Black hair, green eyes (manga) / dark blue eyes (anime)

**Status:** Alive

## Professional Details

**Occupation:** Jujutsu Sorcerer and Student

**Grade:** Grade 2 Jujutsu Sorcerer

**School:** Tokyo Jujutsu High

**Classmates:** Yuji Itadori and Nobara Kugisaki (first-year students)

## Family Relations

**Step-Sibling:** Tsumiki Fushiguro (older step-sister)

**Father:** Toji Fushiguro

**Clan Affiliation:** Descendant of the Zenin Clan (through paternal lineage)

**Notable Relatives:**
- Jinichi Zenin (uncle)
- Maki Zenin (second aunt)
- Mai Zenin (second aunt)
- Naoya Zenin (second uncle)

## Key Background

According to the wiki, Megumi was "born outside the clan as Toji's trump card" and subsequently discovered by Satoru Gojo, who became his mentor and arranged his enrollment at Tokyo Jujutsu High.

## Cursed Technique

**Primary Ability:** Ten Shadows Technique

**Associated Shikigami:** Multiple summoned creatures serving various combat functions

## Personality Traits

The character exhibits a serious, composed demeanor typical of skilled jujutsu practitioners. His thoughtful approach to jujutsu contrasts with more impulsive teammates.

## Character Development Arc

Megumi's narrative involves confronting his complex heritage within the prestigious Zenin Clan while establishing himself as an independent jujutsu sorcerer under Gojo's guidance.

## Voice Acting

**Japanese:** Yuma Uchida (primary); Natsumi Fujiwara (younger version)

**English:** Robbie Daymond
