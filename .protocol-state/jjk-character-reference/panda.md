## Basic Information
- **Name:** Panda (パンダ)
- **Species:** Abrupt-Mutation Cursed Corpse
- **Creator:** Masamichi Yaga (Father figure)
- **Birthday:** March 5
- **Gender:** Male
- **Height:** Over 190 cm (6'3"+)
- **Grade:** Semi-Grade 2
- **Affiliation:** Tokyo Jujutsu High (Second-year student)

## Physical Appearance
Panda resembles "a fully-grown giant panda" with a bear-shaped frame that towers above classmates. He occasionally wears armbands, scarves, and jackets. Following his battle with Hajime Kashimo that destroyed his body and two additional cores, Panda now appears significantly smaller, "resembling a baby panda."

## Core Personality Traits
- **Temperament:** Upbeat and calm with distinctive humor
- **Development:** Initially spoke in formal, computer-like language but evolved a unique personality over time
- **Perspective:** Observes humans as "strange creatures" while respecting them; doesn't aspire to human-like behavior
- **Demeanor:** Sarcastic and uses humor to lighten difficult situations, even during dangerous conflicts

## Special Abilities & Powers

### Physical Prowess
Possesses exceptional strength and combat capabilities befitting his large physique and cursed corpse nature.

### Cursed Energy
Functions as a jujutsu sorcerer with cursed energy manipulation typical of his Semi-Grade 2 classification.

### Multiple Cores System
The character operates through distinct personality cores, including representations associated with Panda, Gorilla, and Triceratops characteristics, providing varied combat approaches.

## Character Relationships
- **Maki Zenin:** Fellow second-year student
- **Toge Inumaki:** Classmate and peer
- **Yuta Okkotsu:** Co-student at Tokyo Jujutsu High
- **Masamichi Yaga:** Creator and parental figure

## Notable Story Arcs & Battles
- Featured prominently in *Jujutsu Kaisen 0: Jujutsu High* as main protagonist
- Significant encounter with Mechamaru involving combat
- Major confrontation with Hajime Kashimo resulting in substantial physical transformation

## Character Development
Panda's evolution from artificial being to developed individual demonstrates growth from strictly programmed behavior to authentic personality expression. His journey represents adaptation from mechanical existence toward genuine social integration within the student community.
