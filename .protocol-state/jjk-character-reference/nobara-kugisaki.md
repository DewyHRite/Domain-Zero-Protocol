## Basic Information
- **Full Name:** Nobara Kugisaki (釘崎野薔薇)
- **Age:** 16 years old
- **Birthday:** August 7th, 2002
- **Gender:** Female
- **Height:** 160 cm (5'3")
- **Hair Color:** Ginger (dyed)
- **Eye Color:** Orange

## Role & Status
- **Position:** Tritagonist of Jujutsu Kaisen series
- **Rank:** Grade 3 Jujutsu Sorcerer
- **School:** Tokyo Jujutsu High (first-year student)
- **Teacher:** Satoru Gojo
- **Classmates:** Yuji Itadori and Megumi Fushiguro

## Physical Appearance
Nobara has mid-length brown hair styled with bangs covering the right side of her forehead, long eyelashes, and thin eyebrows. She maintains "average height with a slim figure" and typically wears the school uniform with a long skirt, black stockings, and brown shoes.

## Family Background
- Unnamed grandmother
- Unnamed mother
- Status: Alive

## Abilities
Her primary cursed technique involves manipulating straw dolls for combat purposes. She operates as an active jujutsu sorcerer capable of handling cursed spirits and participating in school events.

## Professional Details
- **Occupation:** Jujutsu Sorcerer and Student
- **Affiliation:** Tokyo Jujutsu High
- **First Appearance:** Chapter 3 (manga), Episode 2 (anime)

## Voice Acting
- **Japanese:** Asami Seto
- **English:** Anne Yatco
